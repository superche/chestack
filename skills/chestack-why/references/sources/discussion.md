# Team discussion

**Use when:** formal records omit deliberation, rationale or an incident decision.

**Seed:** exact PR/issue URLs, feature vocabulary, symbols or error strings, participants and dates.

## Search and read

1. Search the linked identifier in relevant authorized channels and period. Expand to author, product terms or error text if needed; distinguish a supported query from a filter the tool ignored.
2. Open the full relevant thread, including replies and follow-ups. Trace whether an early suggestion was accepted, rejected or deferred.
3. Follow linked decisions or incident records within scope. When another investigator owns that source, return the lead with its unresolved question.

A joke, acknowledgment or isolated message is not a decision. A later recollection is evidence of that recollection, with weaker proximity to the original choice. Retention, inaccessible channels and private messages outside scope limit coverage; an empty public-channel search does not cover them.

## Return

Channel/thread locator, participants, timestamps, attributed passage and surrounding decision context. Include contrary replies, unavailable thread portions and the exact query/window. Identify whether the record is a proposal, decision or recollection without treating those as interchangeable.
