# Separate investigation from synthesis

**Read when:** the question spans sources, evidence conflicts, or findings are handed to another worker. These are responsibilities, not a fixed agent topology. Inherit the current model. Use independent workers only when available, authorized and useful under the [delegation contract](../../chestack/references/delegation.md); otherwise perform separate collection and synthesis passes without calling them independent.

## Investigation assignment

Provide the original question, code/artifact anchor and revision, decision period, source scope/access limits, known IDs and the relevant category template from [sources](sources.md). Assign ownership of overlapping sources; do not prescribe a preferred explanation. A worker may cover related categories when that avoids broken leads.

The investigator collects and checks records. Preserve source wording when it matters, contrary material and alternate readings. Provisional interpretations belong in a separate field, not inside the evidence quotation. For an investigator-only assignment, stop at the evidence packet and provisional interpretations. The final rationale and preserve / change candidate / avoid / unresolved risk recommendations belong to synthesis, not the collection handoff.

## Evidence packet

Use this compact record per relevant item; fields can remain in working notes for a small sequential task:

- ID, source category, original locator/section, author/date/version and source status.
- Relevant passage or faithful paraphrase, plus what it establishes directly.
- Query/filter/window and whether personally retrieved or supplied; coverage and truncation limits.
- Origin/quotation chain, counterevidence, provisional interpretation and competing reading.
- Cross-source lead: destination/ID, question to resolve, assigned reader or explicit unresolved status.

Finish collection when assigned relevant records and material leads are accounted for, or the precise access/search gap is documented. Return the packet and source coverage, not just a narrative conclusion. A pointer to another worker is not a completed investigation.

## Synthesis assignment

Provide the original question/anchor, all packets including empty/unavailable categories, and access to the underlying authorized sources. The synthesizer owns claim construction, chronology, confidence and the user-facing answer; follow [synthesis](synthesis.md).

Before writing, check packet adequacy: can the material claim be traced to the actual passage and target period? Reopen important originals, merge shared origins, and seek a bounded follow-up for missing context or contradictory claims. A report is a lead, not a substitute for its cited evidence. If retrieval is impossible, mark the claim unverified or downgrade it; do not silently promote a worker's certainty.

Keep a claim-to-evidence map: claim → source IDs → inference step → counterevidence → confidence/gap. Retain unresolved leads that could change the answer. Stop when the question is supported at its stated scope or the next discriminating source is clear. Hand off preserve / change candidate / avoid / unresolved risk with the same qualifications.
