# Calibrate and synthesize rationale

Apply this check even when investigation and writing happen in one pass. Preserve claim-level confidence rather than assigning one label to the whole answer.

| Tier | Required basis | Wording and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | An attributable record explicitly states this reason for this decision | State what the record says with locator and date. It proves recorded intent, not that the choice succeeded or was the sole motive. |
| Supported | Independent evidence converges on the reason and material alternatives have been examined | Show what each source contributes and the inference. Repeated quotations of one source do not add independence. |
| Inferred | A reasoned connection from contextual evidence, with a material inferential gap | State premises and the missing link; use qualified language. Current behavior alone does not identify historical intent. |
| Speculative | A plausible candidate with little discriminating evidence; alternatives fit similarly | Mark it as a possibility and name what would distinguish it. Include only when it helps the question. |
| Unknown | The requested reason cannot be established from available evidence | Name the precise gap and relevant search/access limits. Unknown can coexist with useful hypotheses. |

An explicit rationale comment can be Direct evidence of a stated constraint; verify its revision and scope. Executable code, names and test assertions alone establish mechanics or expectations. A later recollection is direct evidence of that recollection; qualify its distance from the original decision.

## Evidence contract

Keep enough of this record for another pass to inspect your conclusions:

- Question and code/artifact anchor, target revision and decision period.
- Each material claim with tier, supporting locator/passage, source date and role: introduction, repair or present behavior.
- Counterevidence and competing explanations, including what would discriminate between them.
- Source coverage with actual query/scope and found, searched-empty, partial, unavailable or excluded status and reason.
- Open leads and the next useful check. A supplied packet's coverage is not a search you personally executed.

Use short notes for a narrow answer. For contested history, compare candidate explanations against both supporting and opposing evidence. Ask whether the same observation would be expected if a candidate were false; evidence compatible with all candidates cannot select a winner.

## Final audit

1. Reopen material citations and verify the exact statement, target and date. A valid URL/title is insufficient; an inaccessible source remains unverified. Separate retrieved originals from second-hand reports.
2. Merge duplicate evidence origins. Resolve apparent contradictions by version, period or scope where possible; retain genuine disagreements with both citations. Direct records can conflict: report the conflict without silently promoting one to fact.
3. Check that chronology separates original choice, later repair, release and current behavior. A merged fix does not prove deployment or effectiveness.
4. Recheck causal phrasing against each tier. Split a compound claim if the motive is documented but the threshold or outcome is inferred. Preserve null results and access gaps when condensing.
5. For planning, attach evidence and confidence to preserve / change candidate / avoid / unresolved risk. A hypothesis must remain a hypothesis in the handoff; proposing a change does not authorize it.

For mixed-confidence claims, borderline Supported/Inferred judgments, contradictory records or an unexplained threshold, read [calibration examples](calibration.md). The examples illustrate evidence changes; they are not a fixed answer format.
